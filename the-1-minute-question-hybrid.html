    hiii, I'm Lulu ♡ Everyone is so obsessed with becoming famous and claiming their spotlight, but I take pride in making people feel something. Be special. Go outside the box. Everything looks the same — people talk about the same things over and over, copying each other to impress the algorithm. Eventually it's all a washing machine of information.

    I want to make something different, something that actually matters and helps people see beyond. Help people see what's inside themselves — hidden, ready to be discovered, and unlocked from the chains in the dragon's cave.

    fun fact #1 — a childhood dream: this project is a work in progress, born from an idea I had as a kid. It really took off when I spent 7 years traveling the world starting at age 21. Now it's growing into something I hope can be truly useful.

    Did you know that asking powerful mindset optimization questions daily is one of the most significant brain hacks you can add to your routine?

    By consistently asking the questions and exercises you'll get in your inbox, you're priming your brain to seek solutions and focus on the positive. Done daily, you're training your brain to focus on solutions instead of getting stuck in the problem.

    It's fascinating, and it's backed by science, with real benefits for your mental and emotional well-being. It helps you ease stress and overwhelm and can have a profound impact on your confidence. When you become a master at asking yourself the right questions, you feel more in control of your business and your life.

    The scientific evidence behind repetition and active meditation is strong, and the 1-minute daily question uses that knowledge to help you succeed in a simple way — and see results after the very first question.

    One of the most interesting reasons this brain hack works is its ability to trigger a paradigm shift. By asking empowering questions, we train our brain to think differently, focus on possibilities rather than limitations, and see obstacles as opportunities. That shift can change how you approach your business, leading to more innovative and effective strategies.

    How it works

    four tiny steps. no pressure, no streaks to fail.

    1

    join the club

    Pop your email in the welcome box. One question lands in your inbox each day.

    sit with the question

    Read it and let it land — before you write anything. The real impact is what the question brings up.

    3

    answer in one minute

    Don't write the perfect answer, write the honest one. Keep it private in your journal, or share in the guest book.

    come back tomorrow

    Repetition is the magic. Resist the urge to binge many questions at once — one a day is the point.

      about · the 1 minute question

      It's time to start asking the right questions... 🔮

      Did you know that asking powerful mindset optimization questions daily is one of the most significant brain hacks you can incorporate into your daily routine?

      "By doing this on a daily basis, you're essentially training your brain to focus on solutions instead of getting stuck in the problem."

      Not only does this help you eliminate stress and overwhelm, but it can also have a profound impact on your confidence levels. When you become a master at asking yourself the right questions, you begin to feel more in control of your business and your life.

      The scientific evidence behind the benefits of repetition & active meditation is undeniable. This shift in mindset can lead to more innovative and effective strategies.

      Hi, I'm Lulu ♡

      The 1 Minute Question began with a simple idea: most of us don't need more advice, we need a better question at the right moment. So every morning I write one small question — the kind that makes you pause, take a breath and notice what matters today.

      There's no app to keep up with and nothing to catch up on. One question, one minute, and then off you go, maybe lighter, maybe with a plan, maybe with a new idea.

      P.S. The pixels, stars and bubbles are on purpose. I think clarity should feel a little like play.

      3. 3
        Take your 60 seconds

        Answer in your head, in a notebook or out loud. There's no wrong way.

      4. 4
        Carry on with your day

        Feel a little clearer, calmer or more focused. That's the whole trick.
