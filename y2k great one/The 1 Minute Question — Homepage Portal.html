about this place

why this exists, and who's behind it

Did you know that asking powerful mindset questions daily is one of the most significant brain hacks you can add to your routine? By consistently asking the questions you get in your inbox, you're training your brain to seek solutions instead of getting stuck in the problem — and it's backed by science, not just vibes.

When you become a master at asking yourself the right questions, you begin to feel more in control of your life. One of the most interesting reasons this works so well is its ability to trigger a paradigm shift — from limitations to possibilities, from roadblocks to opportunities.

🧚‍♀️ Fun fact #1 — a childhood dream. This project is a work in progress, born from an idea I had as a kid. It really took off when I spent 7 years traveling the world starting at age 21.
🦄 Fun fact #2 — profits. 100% of profit from this project goes right back into development — tools like Stress Bubble Burst, a real-life focus timer, the Thought Rotator, and more.
🧭 how it actually works (read this first)⌄

We know it can be enticing to cut to the chase and just go through many questions, writing answers one after another — but the real impact is experiencing the question and sitting with what it brings up before you start journaling. You'll be amazed by your own brain's power to suddenly see solutions where you once saw roadblocks.

These 1-minute questions help you strengthen thinking patterns backed by real research. If you want to go deeper, look into: neuroplasticity, kaizen, Atomic Habits, the science behind the law of attraction, psycho-cybernetics, and the quiet power of repetition.

Everything here — the spritework, the music, the illustrations, the site itself — was a labor of love. Whenever you need to stop the world for a second and focus on yourself, you're always welcome here. Welcome home. 🪐
